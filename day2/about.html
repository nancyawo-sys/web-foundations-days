About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple app for writing down ideas, reminders and to-do items in seconds. It is built to be fast and distraction-free, so you can capture a thought before it disappears.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the QuickNotes home page.
  2. Type your note into the text box.
  3. Click "Add Note" or press Enter.
  4. Read your notes in the "Your notes" list.

Features

  • Add notes instantly
  • See a live count of your notes
  • Simple, clean layout
  • Works on phones and computers

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Enter Add the note
Tab Move to the next field or button
